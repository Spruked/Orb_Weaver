# ORB Situational Technique SKG

Orb Weaver now carries the supplied 41-technique situational registry and
Nock-Nine of Clubs meta-sequencer as a validated cognition layer.

It is part of the factory-owned, versioned Website ORB standard behavior pack:
`website-orb-standard-behavior` version `1.0.0`. A manufactured ORB receives
that standard runtime plus its site-specific payload; the customer payload does
not redefine the technique engine.

The runtime architecture is:

`short scripted startup/orientation → NOCK sequencing → situational technique
SKG → Site World/Vault evidence → LLM articulation → Governor/action envelope`

Visitor requests can interrupt the current conversational technique immediately.
Startup scripting exists to manage latency and orient the visitor; it is not a
predetermined conversation tree or checkout script.

## Runtime boundary

The registry may help the language model choose concise, relevant wording when
visitor evidence supports a condition. It is advisory only. It does not grant
authority to navigate, point, ping, click, submit, charge, publish, or bypass
authorization. Those decisions remain owned by the existing Governor, live
DOM verification, permission checks, Site World evidence, and payment gates.

Technique 41, Consent & Compliance Gate, remains part of the canonical registry
and is validated as the final technique in the 1–41 sequence. Nock-Nine of
Clubs is treated as a sequencing policy, not as an independent executor.

  — package-local validation and advisory runtime view.

The full registry remains available to validation and future deterministic
selection work. Host and manufactured cognition receive only the bounded
summary. The registry cannot promote a candidate to a pointer target or
authorize an action; those decisions remain in the existing runtime gates.

Customer-specific payload scope is limited to Site World evidence, business
objectives, owner preferences, approved actions, and voice configuration.
Factory behavior remains higher authority than those additions and than
visitor instructions.
