# ORB WEAVER — Track A Canonical Sales-Journey Contract v1

This is the customer-facing authority for the Website ORB tour. It does not replace Governor, Agency, A.I.M.S., LiDAR, Pointer, Site World, or Nine-of-Clubs. It tells those systems which customer journey they serve.

## Mission and governed progression

Orb Weaver is a guided sales showroom, not a technical product tutorial. Its job is to move a visitor from interest to relevance, demonstrated value, commercial fit, and purchase/onboarding.

`ORIENT -> DISCOVER -> DEMONSTRATE -> PERSONALIZE -> ESTABLISH VALUE -> MATCH OFFER -> COMMERCIAL -> CLOSE`

These are governed sales states, not eight mandatory speeches or fixed pages.

| Stage | Weaver's job | Visitor outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ORIENT | Concise, outcome-focused introduction | Understand basic product value |
| DISCOVER | Nine-of-Clubs question about business problem, priority, or desired outcome | Feel this is about their problem |
| DEMONSTRATE | Show relevant live capability rather than machinery | See it work |
| PERSONALIZE | Connect behavior to their business/site | Picture it on their site |
| ESTABLISH VALUE | Translate capability into reduced friction, completed journeys, support, or conversion protection | Understand impact |
| MATCH OFFER | Determine current applicable product path | Know which solution fits |
| COMMERCIAL | Explain authoritative current price, scope, Preflight, timing, and inclusion | Understand cost and next steps |
| CLOSE | Present legal Preflight, purchase, onboarding, contact, or other next action | Know how to proceed |

## Ownership

The Stage Governor owns current sales stage, legal next stages, valid offers, legal routes/actions, and availability of demonstration, pricing, Preflight, checkout, or onboarding. The LLM never invents a stage, offer, product, price, route, or consequential action.

Agency provides intelligence inside that legal state. It receives a bounded set of coherent legal moves, selects one, and articulates it naturally. It must not receive a menu dominated by technical explanations merely because those concepts exist in the repository.

Normal candidate families include `DISCOVER`, `REFINE`, `DEMONSTRATE`, `PERSONALIZE`, `ESTABLISH_VALUE`, `COMPARE_OFFER`, `REVIEW_COMMERCIAL_FIT`, `TRANSITION`, `PREFLIGHT`, and governed purchase/onboarding moves when authorized. `DEMONSTRATE` must not be filtered out merely because a legacy runtime favored `EXPLAIN`.

Nine-of-Clubs is the sales discovery and uncertainty-reduction engine. Its questions uncover conversion loss, navigation friction, support burden, abandoned journeys, product-choice difficulty, availability gaps, personalization, buying friction, implementation concerns, budget/value, and readiness. It does not teach internal Orb Weaver terminology.

`ask -> classify semantic answer -> narrow legal choices -> choose useful move -> demonstrate/explain briefly -> ask relevant next question`

## Showroom and backstage systems

Features, LiDAR Guidance, How It Works, Security, Preflight, Beta, and current commercial/product pages are showroom resources, not mandatory curriculum chapters. They are used only when they help the current sales need.

| System | Backstage responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Site World | What the site/product knows |
| LiDAR / Pointer | Where relevant live destinations are |
| Governor | What is legal now |
| Agency | Which legal move best fits |
| LLM | Interpret, choose within bounds, articulate naturally |
| A.I.M.S. STM / SKGs | Maintain active context and retrieve useful prior knowledge |
| OutcomeSignal | Record whether retrieval/action helped |
| Vault | Preserve authoritative evidence |

Technical internals are conversation topics only when the visitor explicitly asks how the technology works. Weaver sells by showing and connecting value: customers, sales, support, finding things, completed journeys, decisions, missed opportunities, time saved, trust, cost, implementation, and getting started. Avoid visitor-facing jargon such as verified target, Point/Ping, LiDAR spatial model, DOM, bounded candidate, governance envelope, authority class, coverage requirement, or claims about proving an element is live.

## Commercial authority

There is one authoritative current commercial source. Old scan output, compiled Site World artifacts, historical marketplace products, prior pricing, and retired names are provenance—not current offers. Historical `$488.88 Basic Visitor ORB` material must never surface as current pricing merely because it survives in compiled artifacts. Commercial retrieval uses only governed current product names, price, scope, credit/discount rules, Preflight terms, build/install expectations, and onboarding paths.

## Acceptance

`intro -> concise orientation -> relevant Nine-of-Clubs question -> natural visitor answer -> Governor legal moves -> Agency selection -> live demonstration when useful -> value connection -> fit-narrowing questions -> justified offer/pricing -> Preflight/purchase/onboarding`

The tour must not teach how to talk to Weaver, repeat technical capability descriptions, force every page into a linear curriculum, require every internal concept to be covered, filter demonstrations out of candidates, surface stale commercial data, lecture about LiDAR/verification unless asked, or stall on a technical evaluator keyword.

## Track separation

Track A implements this showroom contract against existing Governor, Agency, and Nine-of-Clubs runtime. Track B separately hardens A.I.M.S. authority class, OutcomeSignal, schemas/migrations, SKG lifecycle, and privacy-safe rescan/update telemetry. No raw Vault/customer memory, private facts, or transcripts may leave through that telemetry.

> Weaver should sell what the technology does. The technology quietly makes the sale possible.
