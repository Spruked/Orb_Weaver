Orb Weaver — Master Capability & Verification Architecture

Consolidated from the crawler inspection contract and the August 22 feature inventory. Every atomic item from the July 25 inventory is represented — items were merged only where two documents named the exact same capability, never removed for brevity.

1. Discovery & Crawl Intelligence

Domain/subdomain discovery · sitemap discovery & parsing · robots.txt · route discovery (sitemap-listed, discovered outside sitemap, unresolved) · internal/external link inventory · redirect & redirect-chain detection · broken-link detection · HTTP status collection · canonical URL collection · duplicate/query-string/trailing-slash route variants · orphan-page detection · authenticated/protected route scanning where permitted · route depth & ownership · application-route discovery · API-route inventory · public endpoint mapping · capability endpoint discovery · incremental rescan · targeted (page/route/workflow) rescan · full-site rescan · scan timestamping, versioning, provenance, confidence tracking · scan-result validation, deduplication, normalization, indexing, and persistence · scan audit logging

Crawl lifecycle states: DISCOVERED → RESOLVED → RENDERED → COMPLETE, or explicitly PARTIAL / BLOCKED / FAILED — never silently incomplete.

2. Rendering & Live DOM Intelligence

Per-route render success/failure with cause, not just a count · hydration errors · client-routing failures · missing chunks · console errors, uncaught exceptions, network failures · DOM element inventory (type, ID, classes, attributes, ARIA/accessible name, role) · DOM-anchor collection · visible-label collection · data-attribute collection · visible/hidden/disabled/interactive state · duplicated or unstable/generated selectors · parent-child & region relationships · bounding geometry & viewport status

3. Content & Semantic Intelligence

Content extraction and classification (not scraping) · page-title & meta-description collection · page-purpose classification · page-type classification · headings/sections/content blocks · products, services, prices, features, FAQs, policies, contact/business/location/hours info · terms/privacy/legal-page discovery · documentation & downloadable-file discovery, document metadata collection · image discovery & alt-text collection · video/audio discovery & media metadata collection · structured data discovery: JSON-LD, Schema.org, Open Graph · social-link discovery · search-engine metadata collection · site-search discovery & behavior mapping · terminology, synonyms, aliases, visitor-language mapping · intent-to-page/section/control/workflow mapping · contradiction detection (conflicting prices, hours, phone numbers, warranty language, duplicated-but-different FAQs) · semantic completeness scoring — does the site actually answer the ~12 questions a customer will ask, scored per answered vs. unsupported

4. Design Intelligence

Visual hierarchy · layout/grid analysis · spacing rhythm · typography system & font roles · color system & contrast · brand consistency, brand-name collection, logo & brand-asset discovery, imagery style · component & section-pattern classification · page-template classification · CTA prominence & trust-element placement · responsive/breakpoint behavior, mobile vs. desktop comparison · animation/motion inventory · design-token extraction · visual-signature generation

5. Commerce & Catalog Intelligence

Product/SKU/variant discovery, product-name/description/feature collection · pricing collection & pricing dictionary · availability · specifications & options · category structure · product/service relationships (upsell, cross-sell) · catalog generation · inventory-change tracking (price/stock/variant changes, discontinued items) — locally-compared Tier-3 scan, only escalates to a full rescan on structural (not cosmetic) diffs

6. Interface & Workflow Intelligence

Navigation-menu discovery (header, footer, breadcrumb, sidebar) · buttons, links, CTAs, forms, inputs (text/select/checkbox/radio/textarea), submit/search controls · modals, dialogs, accordions, tabs, dropdowns, carousels, pagination, menu controls · login/signup/account/cart/checkout/contact/download/media controls · full-funnel flow mapping: authentication, signup, login, contact, purchase, cart, checkout, lead-generation · multi-step workflow mapping · required-field & validation-rule detection · error/success/confirmation-state collection · navigation/click/scroll/form action mapping · route-transition & action-result mapping · conversion-path mapping (entry → decision → CTA → destination) · broken/abandoned-path detection

7. LiDAR / Pointer Intelligence

Bounding boxes, screen geometry, viewport position, scroll containers, scroll-target mapping · fixed/sticky elements, overlays, z-order/occlusion · responsive layout & breakpoint geometry changes · pointer-target extraction, pointer-anchor generation, pointer-manifest generation, pointer plot-map generation, target alias generation, target-to-route/section/control mapping

Target verification states (mandatory, not optional): VERIFIED · UNSTABLE · CONFLICT · NOT_FOUND · NOT_INTERACTABLE · REQUIRES_RUNTIME_CHECK · PROTECTED. not started is never a valid completion state.

Broken-target, missing-pointer-target, duplicate-target, and ambiguous-target detection · missing-route/section/control detection.

Recovery pipeline: alternate locator → semantic match → accessibility locator → text match → structural/route-relative locator → DOM reconciliation → outcome recorded as recovered / unresolved / ambiguous / blocked / requires human review.

8. Selective Pointer Authority

semantic_reference vs. live_guidance distinction · guidance eligibility gating · confidence gating before a live Ping · target-loss recovery · no-false-Ping enforcement · candidate correction & promotion path

9. Tesseract Weave (Non-DOM Extraction)

OCR and intelligence extraction from images, PDFs, Office documents, and other non-DOM sources — feeds the same content/semantic/catalog pipelines as live-page extraction

10. Site World / SKG Compilation

Site knowledge graph construction · route/page/section/control/product/service/workflow capsule generation · current-page awareness · physical-navigation map · page-to-page, product-to-page, service-to-page, FAQ-to-page, and control-to-workflow relationship mapping · capability mapping (tool-to-capability, capability-to-route, capability-to-control) · allowed-action mapping · stage & stage-transition mapping · Stage Governor input generation

11. Vault Intelligence

A Priori (verified site knowledge) vs. A Posteriori (learned, promotion-gated) knowledge · structured catalog/pricing stores · interaction memory & verified outcomes · verified-site-state storage · semantic retrieval · self-pruning, merge/compress/weaken/retire logic

12. TPC / Runtime Cognition

Deterministic fast-path answers from Vault/catalog · LLM escalation only when required · intent recognition · contextual articulation · capability selection (Governed by the existing cognitive-engine/Doctrine architecture — this system consumes Site World/Vault output, it doesn't duplicate the crawl.)

13. Voice & Visitor Guidance

STT/TTS, browser playback, voice selection & fallback, listening/speaking state, interruption handling, low-latency response · guided focus, scroll/move/highlight · visitor-approved actions · MORB-assisted guidance · safe handoff and recovery when guidance fails

14. ORB Personality & Physical Design

Factory-default ORB, skins, opacity/motion doctrine, animation, behavior packs, voice packs, sound effects, idle/presence/speech behavior, custom identity

15. Manufacturing Pipeline

Project → weave → Site World → catalog → pointer map → A Priori compile → owner policy → skin → voice → behavior → Vault → runtime assembly → release manifest. Site-specific ORB knowledge, tool, pointer, navigation, and workflow configuration · articulation-context and capability-snapshot generation.

16. Dock Station

Owner-facing configuration: appearance, behavior, conversation review, intelligence, speech, tools, profiles, deployment, statistics, diagnostics, Live Test entry point

17. Live Test & Release QA

Site identity & domain binding · Site World binding · pointer integrity · voice · cognition · Vault isolation (customer-data isolation confirmed) · policy compilation · skin fallback · secrets check · release validation

18. Release & Deployment

Downloadable ORB package · compiled-site-package, ORB deployment-data, and ORB update-data / website-intelligence-package generation · release versioning & manifest · universal loader script · install snippet · runtime endpoint binding · updates and rollback

19. Marketplace

Skins, voices, behavior packs, sound packs, enhancements, services, and upgrade packages — recurring revenue surface

20. Lifecycle & Self-Maintenance

New/removed/changed page, control, form, navigation, and workflow detection · stale-content/stale-route detection · Site World & pointer-map refresh · learned-correction promotion · self-pruning

21. Auditing & Reporting

Crawl-report generation · endpoint-map generation · SEO (titles, meta, canonicals, headings, indexability, structured data, internal linking) · Accessibility (labels, ARIA, keyboard nav, focus order, contrast where measurable) · Technical Health (HTTP/JS/CSP/CORS/API/asset failures) · Performance (load timing, asset weight, blocking resources, LCP/CLS/INP/TTFB where instrumentable) · Security/trust signals (HTTPS, mixed content, exposed secrets, insecure forms/cookies, CSP presence) — explicitly not a penetration-testing tool · Design audit · Pointer/route/control/workflow audit

22. Manufacturing Tiers & Sibling Product

Basic / Enhanced / Platinum manufacturing configurations, increasing intelligence, customization, and guidance depth · Web Weaver — same intelligence stack, used to design/build a new site when no existing site is worth upgrading

Owner-Priority View (business value, not build order)

Tier 1 — money & customers: conversion blockers, broken functionality, search visibility, customer friction, missing/confusing information Tier 2 — trust: contradictions, accessibility, security/trust signals, performance, outdated content Tier 3 — opportunity: content gaps, internal linking, visitor guidance quality, sales-flow quality, ORB readiness

ORB-Priority View (what the runtime needs to function safely)

Route truth → live DOM truth → target verification → geometry/LiDAR truth → pointer recovery → knowledge grounding → semantic relationships → Governor state → runtime health → evidence behind every claim

Crawl Completion Contract

A crawl is not COMPLETE because pages were fetched. It requires: routes discovered & resolved, required routes rendered or failures explicitly recorded, content extracted & classified, DOM extracted, pointer candidates extracted, runtime verification attempted, unresolved targets processed through recovery, baseline SEO + accessibility inspection done, technical-health errors collected, ORB-readiness check completed, and every subsystem returning a status + evidence.

States: CRAWL COMPLETE (all required subsystems succeeded) · CRAWL PARTIAL (a subsystem couldn't run) · CRAWL BLOCKED (a dependency prevents completion) · CRAWL FAILED (execution itself failed).

Six Authoritative Rollup Outputs

Every crawl compiles into exactly these, giving both the owner and the ORB what they need from one source of truth:

  1. Website Condition — what's broken or unhealthy
  2. Search & Discovery — can people and search engines find and understand it
  3. Customer Experience — can visitors understand and navigate it
  4. Commercial Opportunity — can visitors convert
  5. ORB Readiness — can ORB safely understand, guide, answer, point, and act
  6. Evidence & Verification — can every finding be proven

Future / Explicitly-Gated Expansion

Not baseline scope — requires additional permission or integration before use, and should stay clearly separated from the core audit:

  • Competitive intelligence (leaves the site — requires explicit external-research permission)
  • Behavioral analytics (bounce/scroll/rage-click/funnel data — requires analytics access)
  • Search Console integration (impressions/CTR/position — distinguishes "SEO looks weak" from "this page actually lost 38% of impressions")
  • Policy/compliance rule engines (industry-specific disclosure/warranty/financing language) — must run on explicit rules, never generic LLM judgment
