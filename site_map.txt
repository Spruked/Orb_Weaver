ROUTE OWNERSHIP
---------------
Public routes are rendered before authentication in App.tsx. Signed-in routes render inside
Layout.tsx and use the workspace navigation. Unknown signed-out paths fall back to AuthPage;
unknown signed-in paths remain inside the authenticated route shell without a catch-all page.
